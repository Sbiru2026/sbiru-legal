2. WHAT SBIRÙ IS FOR

To use Sbirù, including its basic functions, you need to create a free account (see section 3.1): the App cannot be used anonymously. With the free account you can:

  • scan the barcode of a food, cosmetic or pet product to get a health score based on ingredients, nutritional values and any safety recalls;
  • search for products by name;
  • receive suggestions for alternative products with a better score, including through Amazon affiliate links;
  • contribute to the database by uploading photos/labels of products not yet in the database, a feature that awards a public "badge" and Sbirù+ trial days.

The optional Sbirù+ subscription (see section 3.2), whose prices and conditions will be defined and communicated to users when available, unlocks additional features, including:

  • saving a personal "fitness" profile to receive personalized scores and alerts on sugars, fats and other nutrients calculated based on your current weight and target weight;
  • saving one or more "pet" profiles to receive personalized scores for pet food;
  • choosing specific nutrients to monitor in scanned products.

This descriptive section helps you understand why we collect certain data; the details of each purpose are explained further below.

3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

3.1 Data you provide when creating an account (mandatory)

To use any feature of Sbirù, including basic scanning and product search, you need to create an account: the App cannot be used anonymously. The basic account is free; some additional features require the optional Sbirù+ subscription (see section 3.2). To create the account we collect:

  • email address;
  • first and last name;
  • password (never stored in plain text: it is transformed with the scrypt cryptographic function and a random salt unique to each user, so not even we can retrieve the original password);
  • account creation date;
  • a session token, valid for 30 days, that keeps you logged in.

3.2 Sbirù+ subscription data

To manage the free trial period and any Sbirù+ subscription (premium features) we retain: the trial start date, the date until which the subscription is active, and any bonus days earned by contributing to the product database. As of the date of this Policy, Sbirù does not directly process card payments or other financial instruments within the App; access to premium features occurs through the free trial and/or bonus days.

3.3 Preferences on nutrients to monitor

If you have an active Sbirù+ subscription, you can choose to be alerted about the presence of specific nutrients (e.g. salt, sugars, saturated fat) in scanned products. This list of preferences is associated with your account.

3.4 Fitness profile and personal nutrition plan (optional)

If you activate the fitness profile to receive personalized scores, you can provide: sex (or "prefer not to say"), age, current weight, height, target weight, goal (lose weight / maintain weight / gain muscle mass) and physical activity level. Your current weight and target weight are also used to generate personalized alerts when you scan a product with a notable content of sugars, fats or other nutrients relative to your goal. This is data relating to health and physical characteristics: we process it only with your explicit consent, given by voluntarily activating this feature, and you can delete it at any time from your profile settings.

3.5 Pet profile (optional)

If you add a profile for a pet, you can provide: the pet's name, species (dog/cat/other), breed, age, target weight, activity level, any known allergies (free-text field) and additional notes. This data concerns your pet, not you, but we treat it with the same care as personal data when it is associated with your account.

3.6 Content you upload to contribute to the database

If a product is not in the database, you can contribute by uploading a photo of the packaging and/or the ingredient list, or by entering the ingredient text manually. Images are processed to be compressed/resized and to extract text via optical character recognition (OCR), performed locally on the server or through a third-party OCR service (see section 5). Uploaded photos and text are visible to our moderation team for approval and may become part of the public product database after review. Do not upload images containing your own or third parties' personal data (e.g. recognizable people, documents, receipts with payment data): such images would still be processed like any other uploaded content and could be rejected during moderation.

3.7 "Community" activity and public visibility

If you contribute new products, the number of contributions you have made generates a level "badge". Contributors' public name and badge may appear on a leaderboard visible to other users of the App. If you do not want your name to appear on the leaderboard, do not use the contribution feature, or contact us to discuss showing an alternative name.

3.8 Diagnostic and error data

To identify and fix malfunctions, the App automatically sends our server a report when an unexpected technical error occurs in the browser. The report may include: error message, stack trace, URL of the page where the error occurred, code line/column, and the browser's "user agent" string (browser/operating system type and version). This data is used exclusively for technical maintenance purposes.

3.9 Automatically collected data

When you use Sbirù, our server automatically receives your IP address, which is necessary for the internet to function. We use it for two limited purposes: (a) approximately determining your country/language in order to show you the App in the correct language on first use, and (b) applying limits on the number of requests to prevent abuse and automated attacks (rate limiting). We do not keep a permanent, searchable log of IP addresses linked to your identity, except as strictly necessary for technical security and for the minimum time required for that purpose.

3.10 Data stored locally on your device

Some information stays only in your browser, via the "local storage" feature, and is not sent to our servers except when it needs to be synced with an account: history of scanned products, number of scans performed, preferred language, whether you have already seen the onboarding screens, any personal nutrition plan (current weight and target weight) used to generate alerts on scanned products, and a local copy of fitness/pet profiles for faster access. You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser's browsing data for the Sbirù site.

3.11 Camera

To scan barcodes, the App requests access to your device's camera through the browser. The image captured by the camera is processed locally in the browser solely to recognize the framed barcode: we do not record or transmit camera video to our servers, except when you voluntarily choose to take and upload a photo to contribute to the database (see section 3.6).

4. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (GDPR)

We process your data for the following purposes and legal bases:

  • Creating and managing your account (mandatory to use the App), providing the scanning and product-scoring service, authenticating you and keeping the session active: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Managing the free trial and Sbirù+ subscription: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Providing personalized scores and alerts based on the fitness profile or pet profile: explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and, for health-related data, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the relevant data from your profile.
  • Enabling contributions to the database and their moderation: performance of the contract and legitimate interest in maintaining data accuracy (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Showing public leaderboards of contributors: implicit consent given by activating the contribution feature, with the option not to use it.
  • Identifying and fixing technical errors (diagnostic data): legitimate interest in keeping the Service working and secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Preventing abuse through IP-based rate limiting: legitimate interest in the security of the Service (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Complying with legal obligations where applicable: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

We do not use your data for behavioral advertising, nor do we sell it to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. SHARING DATA WITH THIRD PARTIES

To operate Sbirù we rely on some external providers, who act as processors on our behalf or as independent controllers for data you yourself choose to send them:

  • Supabase (database and authentication): hosts the database of accounts, fitness/pet profiles, preferences and sessions. See Supabase's privacy policy for details on their processing.
  • Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts: open databases from which we retrieve product information (ingredients, nutritional values, brands). Requests to these services concern the product searched for and do not require or transmit your personal data.
  • USDA FoodData Central: a US public database used to enrich products' nutritional information; here too, no personal data of the user is transmitted.
  • Amazon (Product Advertising API / affiliate program): when we suggest an alternative product, the link may be an Amazon affiliate link. If you click on such a link you will be redirected to the Amazon website, which will process your data according to its own privacy policy; we only receive a possible commission, not your purchase data.
  • Third-party optical character recognition (OCR) service (OCR.space): when you upload a photo of a label to contribute to the database, the image may be sent to this external service to extract the text. Do not upload images containing personal data unrelated to the product.
  • The FDA's (US Food and Drug Administration) public safety recall database: used to check whether a scanned product matches a known recall; this does not involve sending the user's personal data, only the product's name/brand.
  • Our hosting and technical infrastructure provider, necessary to deliver the Service.

We do not share your data with advertisers and do not use advertising tracking tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) within the App. The App only loads Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com): requesting these fonts involves sending your IP address to Google according to standard web technical practice.

We may disclose your data to competent authorities if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of users.

6. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Some of the providers listed in section 5 (e.g. Supabase, Amazon, OCR.space) may process data on servers located outside the European Union, including in the United States. In such cases, the transfer takes place on the basis of the appropriate safeguards provided for by the GDPR made available by these providers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms recognized by applicable law.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the Service:

  • account data remains until you request account deletion or until the account is inactive for a prolonged period (normally 24 months of inactivity);
  • fitness profile and pet profile data remains until you delete it yourself or delete the account;
  • sessions automatically expire after 30 days;
  • diagnostic error data is retained for as long as necessary for technical diagnosis and is then deleted or aggregated anonymously;
  • content uploaded to contribute to the product database (photos, text) may be retained even after account deletion, in a form anonymous with respect to you, since it becomes part of a shared product database no longer traceable to your identity once the link with the account is removed.

Upon account deletion, your identifying personal data (email, first name, last name, password, linked profiles) is deleted from our database, except for what is retained for legal obligations or for the purposes described above relating to contributions already published in the shared database.

8. DATA SECURITY

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including: password hashing with scrypt and a random salt unique to each user (the plain-text password is never stored); HTTP security headers via the Helmet library; rate limiting to prevent automated attacks; sessions with an expiration time. No system is 100% secure: in the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will inform you as required by applicable law.
