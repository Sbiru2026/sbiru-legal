How it works

1

Scan the barcode

Open Sbirù and point the camera at the product, in the shop or at home.

2

Get the score

A score from 0 to 100 and a clear rating: Excellent, Good, Mediocre or Poor.

3

See why

Find out what raises or lowers the score: ingredients, additives, processing, nutrition.

Three categories, one glance

Food

Nutrition values, level of processing and additives. Honey has its own dedicated scoring that looks at origin, purity and processing.

Cosmetics

Every ingredient on the INCI list assessed one by one, so you know what goes on your skin.

Pet food

A score designed for dogs and cats, also based on your pet’s profile.

A transparent score

It is not a random number: the score follows public criteria and open data, and every result shows the reasons behind it. When product information is missing, the app says so instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Sbirù is free to use. There is also a Plus subscription with extra features, purchased through Google Play.

Where does the product data come from?

From open databases such as Open Food Facts and from information added by the community. If a product is missing, you can help us add it.

What about alcoholic drinks?

They are not scored: the app does not give a health score to alcohol.

Does the score replace a doctor’s advice?

No. Sbirù is an information tool and does not give medical or dietary advice.
